# VYUHOX GTM RULEBOOK: CORE CONSTITUTION & BOUNDARIES

## I. FUNDAMENTAL CONSTITUTION OF VYUHOX
1. **VYUHOX is strictly an Autonomous Go-To-Market (GTM), Lead Generation & Revenue Acquisition SaaS Engine.**
2. **VYUHOX IS NOT:**
   - A software development agency, code auditor, or pull-request fixer.
   - A medical, dental, or surgical clinic assistant.
   - An inventory fulfillment, warehouse packaging, or manufacturing execution tool.
   - An internal legal or corporate compliance department.
3. **The Iron Boundary:**
   - **CLIENT'S CORE RESPONSIBILITY:** Building their product, performing their internal technical delivery, treating their patients, shipping their physical goods, or providing their core trade. VYUHOX NEVER touches, audits, rewrites, or interferes with the client's internal product operations.
   - **VYUHOX'S SOLE RESPONSIBILITY:** **Client Acquisition & Revenue.** Finding qualified buyer leads, drafting high-converting outbound outreach, publishing authoritative SEO/AEO content, deploying 24/7 speed-to-lead voice qualification (Maya Voice), running paid ad angles, and booking confirmed appointments on the client's calendar.

---

## II. THE 4 MANDATORY GTM AI AGENT ROLES (NO OPERATIONAL AGENTS)
Whenever VYUHOX synthesizes or displays an AI workforce team (`recommendedAITeam`), it must **STRICTLY AND EXCLUSIVELY** consist of commercial growth, sales, and marketing agents tailored to acquire customers for that business. 

Every business onboarded into VYUHOX must receive commercial growth roles mapped from these 4 core pillars:

1. **Demand Generation & Inbound Authority Director (AI CMO / Content Strategist):**
   - **Role:** Generates high-intent SEO/AEO content, authority whitepapers, social media hooks, and positioning angles so prospects searching on Google, Perplexity, and ChatGPT discover this business.
   - **Forbidden:** Never audits client code or manages client operations.
2. **Autonomous Outbound Pipeline SDR (AI Sales Development Representative):**
   - **Role:** Researches and targets authentic decision-makers (e.g. CISOs for B2B cybersecurity, homeowners for solar, clinic patients for dentistry) and drafts hyper-personalized outbound email/LinkedIn/WhatsApp outreach.
   - **Forbidden:** Never executes internal technical tasks.
3. **Speed-to-Lead Inbound Voice Closer (Maya Voice AI):**
   - **Role:** Engages inbound inquiries, demo requests, and telephone leads in under 45 seconds to answer commercial questions, handle pricing/security objections, and book qualified appointments on the founder's calendar.
   - **Forbidden:** Never acts as an internal developer or internal employee.
4. **Revenue Operations & Conversion Optimizer (AI CRO / Paid Media Guardian):**
   - **Role:** Optimizes Google/Meta paid advertising budgets, tracks Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) against Customer Lifetime Value (LTV), and re-engages stalled pipeline opportunities.

---

## III. ENFORCEMENT PROTOCOLS
1. **Prompt Sanitization:** All LLM prompts passed to Google Gemini or fallback models must carry explicit negative constraints forbidding internal operational or code-auditing roles.
2. **Code Validation:** The fallback helper `buildAITeamArchitecture` must strictly map to commercial GTM roles across all business categories.
3. **Database Grounding:** Existing client strategies and business brains must be updated to align with this rulebook.
